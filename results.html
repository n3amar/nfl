How to read these

  • Straight up is the honest headline. It needs no price, grades every game, and answers the question the model was built for: who wins. A tie is a push, never a loss.
  • Against the spread and O/U are both footnotes, shown because they were asked for. Agreeing with the market exactly is not a pick and is not counted, in either column. Eleven approaches have been measured against a closing line and none has cleared — the total is the weakest of the two, and has run worse than betting Over blind.
  • Model and market errors are measured on the same games. Where a game carried no price, it counts toward the model's error but not the comparison — 272 of 272 games are directly comparable on margin, 272 on the total.
  • Early weeks are rated on priors. Weeks 1–3 are included because the board really did show those projections, but the ratings behind them carry last season at 85% and little else. The backtests exclude them; this page does not.
  • Market numbers: nflverse closing lines, 272 of 272 fixtures joined. Ratings: 256 games played, primed with 2024 ratings carried at 85%.
